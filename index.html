About

A short introduction

My research interests sit at the intersections of several fields rather than within a single discipline. I enjoy following questions across disciplinary boundaries - learning the tools of a field, connecting them with ideas from another, and developing those intersections into research problems and projects.

I have worked primarily with machine learning, causal inference, and bioinformatics, and am currently exploring broader directions including NLP, speech processing, reinforcement learning, continual learning, and procedural generation.

I learn through making connections between ideas, disciplines, and people. I teach to learn, and so I learn by connecting.
