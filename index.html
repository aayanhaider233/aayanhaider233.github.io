Featured Research

From DMRs to
Disease Diagnosis

A Causal and Inferential Framework for Schizophrenia Using DNA Methylation

My undergraduate thesis investigated whether hierarchical analysis of DNA methylation could identify biologically meaningful causal relationships associated with disease.

The framework combined differential methylation analysis, differentially methylated regions, gene mapping, WGCNA, causal discovery, and machine learning into a hierarchical feature-selection pipeline.
